Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android apps Hex Fit, Block Jam and Noodle Stall (together, the "Apps").
Publisher: Tonobity ("we", "us"). Effective date: 2026-11-14.

In short: the Apps have no accounts and do not ask for your name or email. Your game progress is stored on your device. The Apps show ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and approximate location to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Tonobity publishes the Apps. For questions about this policy or to exercise your privacy rights, email the developer email address shown on the App’s Google Play store page.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information we do not collect

We do not run user accounts, do not ask you to sign in, and do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or precise location. The Apps do not contain a third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.

2.2 Data stored on your device

The Apps store game data locally on your device using the app's local storage (for example your progress, best score, coins, purchases you have made, ad-free status, settings, and timestamps used for timers and offline earnings). This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. The Apps also keep a short event log in memory (such as "level completed" or "ad shown") that is used only on the device to decide when ads and offers appear. It is not uploaded to us or to any analytics service.

2.3 Data collected by Google AdMob

The Apps use Google AdMob to show interstitial and rewarded video ads. When ads are requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK, operated by Google, may collect and use data from your device, including:

  • Advertising ID (and other device identifiers such as app instance identifiers);
  • Device and software information (for example device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (not precise GPS location);
  • Ad interaction and app usage information (for example which ads were shown, clicked or completed) and diagnostics such as crash logs and performance data.

Google uses this data to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the SDK working. We do not receive this data about you as an individual from Google. Google acts under its own privacy policy. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some other regions), the Apps display Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform, "UMP") before ads are requested. Personalised ads are only shown if you consent where consent is required. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will be non-personalised, and in some regions ads may not be shown at all.

2.4 Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details, and we never see or store your card or bank details. We receive from Google Play only the confirmation that a product was purchased, which the App uses to unlock the item on your device.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game and keep your progress on your device;
  • To show ads (via AdMob) that fund the free Apps, and to let you remove forced ads by purchase;
  • To deliver and restore items you bought;
  • To respond to support requests you send us by email.

4. Advertising choices

  • In the App: open the in-game Shop and choose "Privacy choices" to review or change your consent (shown where applicable).
  • On Android: go to Settings > Privacy (or Google > Ads) to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • Remove ads: the optional "remove ads" purchase stops ads that appear on their own. Rewarded ads you choose to watch remain available as opt-in features.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone except as described here: advertising data is processed by Google through the AdMob SDK, and purchases are processed by Google Play. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

Because the Apps have no accounts and keep your game data only on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings > Apps > (App name) > Storage > Clear data, or by uninstalling the App. We hold no server-side copy of your game data to delete. For data held by Google (advertising and Google Play), see Google's policies below and your Google Account controls at myaccount.google.com.

7. Security

Network traffic between the Apps and Google's ad and billing services uses encryption in transit. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

8. Children

The Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the Apps in a way that conflicts with this policy, contact us at the developer email address shown on the App’s Google Play store page and we will help; deleting the App's data removes what is stored on the device.

9. Your rights

EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (for example through the in-app "Privacy choices" option). You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. The legal bases we rely on are your consent (for personalised advertising) and our legitimate interest in running and funding the Apps (for non-personalised advertising and fraud prevention). Because we do not hold personal data about you on our own servers, most requests are satisfied by clearing the App's data; Google acts as an independent controller for data processed through AdMob.

California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws). You may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. The advertising identifiers processed by AdMob may count as "sharing" under some state laws; you can opt out through the in-app "Privacy choices" option or your Android ad settings. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To make a request, email the developer email address shown on the App’s Google Play store page. We may need to verify that a request is genuine; since we hold no account data, we may be unable to link a request to a particular device.

10. International transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries outside your own, including the United States, under its own safeguards. See Google's policies for details.
